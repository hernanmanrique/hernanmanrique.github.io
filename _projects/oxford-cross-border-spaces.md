Minerva Global Security Programme project examining contested cross-border spaces where armed state and non-state actors cooperate and compete in governing territory. The project investigates how illicit flows of weapons, drugs, money, and trafficked persons intersect with regional instability across the Horn of Africa, the Middle East, the Andean Region, the Golden Triangle, South Asia, Ukraine, and Russia.

Funded by the Minerva Research Initiative in partnership with the Air Force Office of Scientific Research (Award #FA9550-22-1-0338), October 2021 – September 2025, led by Prof. Annette Idler at the University of Oxford's Global Security Programme, in collaboration with George Washington University, George Mason University, and Tufts University.

Hernán's contribution is funded through the project's XCEPT-supported work on illicit flows and regional order.
