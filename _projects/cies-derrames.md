Study on how petroleum spills affect maternal and child health in Loreto, Peru, incorporating a gender perspective. Used an interdisciplinary methodology combining qualitative and quantitative methods, including fieldwork at the Cuninico Native Community. Identified direct health impacts and three indirect consequences: disruption of food security, emotional distress among women, and intensified gender-based labor division.

With Fiorella Parra and Vania Martínez, affiliated with Universidad del Pacífico. Funded by Global Affairs Canada, IDRC, the Manuel J. Bustamante De la Fuente Foundation, SUNAT, and CIES (2018 Annual Research Competition). 14-month project, published 2019.
